# 45-Second Trailer Plan

The trailer must contain both generated material and real gameplay capture.

| Time | Shot | Source | On-screen text/audio |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Fast portal reveal and spike flash | Generated key art/image-to-video | “FIVE CRYSTALS.” |
| 3–7s | Player facing the arena | Generated establishing shot | “THREE LIVES.” |
| 7–15s | Running and first crystal pickup | Real gameplay | Pickup sound; “COLLECT” |
| 15–23s | Crossing spike field and losing health | Real gameplay | Damage sound; “SURVIVE” |
| 23–31s | Rapid crystal pickup montage | Real gameplay | Music builds |
| 31–37s | Portal unlocks and player enters | Real gameplay | Win sound |
| 37–45s | Title card and cover art | Generated key art + title | “CRYSTAL ESCAPE — PLAY NOW” and URL |

## Hook copy

```text
FIVE CRYSTALS.
THREE LIVES.
ONE WAY OUT.
```

## Key-art prompt starter

```text
Cinematic game key art for “Crystal Escape,” a lone white explorer
in a dark neon-violet arena, five cyan magical crystals floating
around dangerous red spike traps, a brilliant circular escape portal
in the distance, stylized low-poly 3D game aesthetic, dramatic rim
light, strong centered composition, no text, 16:9
```

Final generated key art: `KeyArt_CrystalEscape.png`. The exact production prompt is recorded in `Documentation/PROMPT_LOG.md`.

## Capture checklist

- Record at 1080p if possible.
- Hide the mouse cursor.
- Capture a clean reward, damage, loss, win, and restart.
- Do not represent generated shots as gameplay.
- Put the playable URL on the final title card.
- Save the final file or public link in this folder/README.
