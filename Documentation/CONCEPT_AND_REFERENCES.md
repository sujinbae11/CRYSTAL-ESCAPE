# Concept, Mood, and References

## Concept inspiration

Crystal Escape is designed as a readable, compact arcade challenge rather than a large exploration game. The central idea is a three-part loop the player can understand immediately: collect five bright objects, avoid red danger zones, and reach a visually dominant exit.

## Gameplay references

- Small 3D collectathons: readable pickups distributed around a single navigable arena.
- Time-trial obstacle courses: fast restarts and a visible completion time.
- Arcade survival games: three health points, strong hit feedback, and an unambiguous loss state.

These are genre-level references, not imported game assets. Add exact titles, links, screenshots, and image-license/source information if a visual moodboard is used in the final README.

## Target visual style

- Dark midnight-blue arena with violet structures.
- Cyan for rewards and the safe exit.
- Red/magenta for damage and danger.
- Stylized low-poly Tripo props with strong silhouettes.
- Minimal HUD using large, high-contrast type.

## Target sound

- Quiet mysterious synth loop that leaves room for gameplay feedback.
- Bright glassy chime for rewards.
- Short distorted low impact for damage.
- Rising tonal cue for victory and descending cue for loss.
