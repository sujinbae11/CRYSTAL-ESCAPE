This reflection must describe your actual decisions and experience. The questions below are a worksheet, not a finished submission. After you test and modify the game, replace this entire file with a truthful 200–300 word reflection.

Address all of these points:

1. What did the AI generate or suggest?
2. What did you personally decide, test, place, revise, or reject?
3. What failed, and how did you recover?
4. How did working in Unity change the division of labor compared with Portfolio Game 2?

Suggested structure:

- Why did you choose a five-crystal escape loop?

- Codex: gameplay scaffold, C# scripts, debugging suggestions, documentation structure.

## Paragraph 3 — What you did

- Selecting generations, importing models, checking scale and collision.
- Testing controls, tuning difficulty, evaluating errors, recording footage.
- Any code, layout, material, timing, or audio changes you personally made.

## Paragraph 4 — PG2 comparison

- Explain why a game engine required more integration and testing.
- Discuss how AI could produce pieces but could not automatically verify the final player experience.
